# Humaaans parts

Mix-and-match figure parts by Pablo Stanley — 18 heads, 10 torsos, 11 leg sets,
shoes and scene props, each a separate SVG.

- Source: the `docs/objects` set of [ozgrozer/hdt](https://github.com/ozgrozer/hdt) (MIT),
  which packages the parts from [humaaans.com](https://www.humaaans.com) (CC0).
- Both licences permit commercial use; CC0 requires no attribution. This notice
  is here because crediting the author is the decent thing to do, not a condition.

They are used because drawing people by typing path coordinates produced
competent but awkward figures. These were drawn by a designer and are already
modular, so the work is stacking and rigging them rather than drawing them.
